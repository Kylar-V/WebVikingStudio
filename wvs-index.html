About the Viking

I'm Kai — the one-man crew behind Web Viking Studio. Before this, I spent years in hospitality, which taught me a lot about what makes a local business actually run: word of mouth, repeat customers, and being easy to find.

These days I build websites for small local businesses that don't have one yet — places that are trusted by everyone who already knows them, but invisible to everyone who doesn't. A good website fixes that.

No agency, no account managers, no jargon — just a straightforward build, a fair price, and someone you can actually talk to.
